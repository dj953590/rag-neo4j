Financial Reporting : Documents that disclose a company’s financial condition and performance, including the subcategory '10-Ks' for Annual Report, '10-Qs' for Quarterly Report, and audited financial statements. Used to assess creditworthiness.
Legal Agreements : Binding contracts that define the terms of a credit relationship, such as subcategory of 'credit agreements', 'loan agreements', and 'fee letters'. Core to understanding rights and obligations.
Risk Management : Documents related to mitigating lender risk, such as subcategory of 'guarantees', 'letters of credit', and 'insurance policies'. They offer protection in case of borrower default.
Credit Approval : Internal and external documents used to support underwriting decisions, including subcategory of 'borrowing base certificates', 'covenant compliance reports', and 'internal memos'.
Collateral Documentation : Documents that describe or perfect security interests in pledged assets, such as subcategory of 'UCC filings', 'mortgages', 'appraisals', and 'lien certificates'.
Regulatory & Compliance : Documents required to meet legal and regulatory standards, such as subcategory of 'KYC forms', 'OFAC certifications', and 'FR Y-14', 'CECL support').
Supporting / Correspondence : Additional materials such as subcategory 'legal opinions', 'board resolutions', 'emails', and 'internal notes' that clarify or support the credit file or transaction.
