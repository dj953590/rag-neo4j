company: A legal business entity or corporation
corporation: A specific type of company with limited liability
agreement: A legal contract outlining terms between parties
amendment: A modification or addition to an existing agreement
commitment: A lender's obligation to provide financing
executed date: The date on which the agreement was signed
maturity date: The final due date for repayment of a loan
waiver: A legal decision to waive a contractual obligation
borrower: The entity receiving the loan under the agreement
guarantor: An entity that guarantees the borrower's obligations
obligor: A party legally bound to fulfill obligations under a contract
lender: A financial institution providing the loan
secured party: A lender holding a security interest in collateral
agent: An entity managing administrative loan functions
facility: A structured credit or loan arrangement
swingline: A short-term borrowing facility
letter of credit: A guarantee from a bank ensuring payment
base rate benchmark: A reference interest rate (e.g. SOFR, LIBOR)
interest rate: The cost of borrowing expressed as a percentage
margin: The additional percentage added to the base rate
default interest: The increased interest rate applied in case of default
collateral: Assets pledged to secure a loan
security trustee: An entity holding security interests on behalf of lenders
lien: A legal right over a borrower's assets as collateral
covenants: Financial and operational restrictions on the borrower
schedule: A structured timeline for loan events
prepayment: The ability to repay a loan before maturity
syndicated: A loan provided by multiple lenders
assignee: A party receiving loan rights via transfer
transferor: A lender transferring its loan interest
agency: The function of managing a syndicated loan
regulatory: Entities responsible for overseeing compliance and regulations
