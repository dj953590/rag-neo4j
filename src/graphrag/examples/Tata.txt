Tata Technologies (TATE.NS) 


Initiate at Sell: Leveraged to Auto Vertical; Upsides Priced In 



CITI'S TAKE 
Initiate at Sell; TP of Rs1000 
– Tata Technologies (TTL) is a ER&D service 
provider (particularly exposed to automotive vertical) and will likely benefit 
from (a) resilience of tech spends in the automotive vertical given its 
specialization and expertise (b) focus on other key verticals like aerospace – 
recently, TTL has become a strategic supplier for Airbus (c) Mining 
opportunities in existing set of marquee 
clients – TML, JLR, etc. Tata 
Group 
parentage will further help. While we are constructive on the business 
medium term, valuations (~53x 1-yr forward Citi ests) appear to be pricing 
in the positives. Initiate at Sell with TP of Rs1,000 (~10% downside). 


Multiple growth drivers — (a) Global OEMs are increasing their R&D spends across 
the broader theme of ‘ACES’ technologies – autonomous, connected, electrification 
and shared. TTL offers a one stop platform for automotive OEMs to meet new 
engineering needs across the value chain (b) TTL benefits from the strong track 
record & reputation of its promoter TML, part of the Tata Group – TTL will likely 
leverage their parent relationship to acquire newer clients (c) Focus on aerospace 
vertical – Airbus ramp up and business from group company “Air India” to be 
monitored (d) Mining opportunities in existing set of marquee clients – TML, JLR, etc. 


Can margins improve? — TTL has improved EBIT margins from ~13% in FY20 to 
16.5% in FY23. Still, TTL has additional margin levers – (a) pyramid optimization (b) 
higher offshore mix (c) operating leverage given decent growth. Wage hikes, scarcity 
of some specialized skill in niche areas will offset some of the benefit. Net-net, we 
are expecting some EBIT margin improvement in FY24E and FY25E. 


Key areas to focus — (a) Will the ER&D spends in automotive vertical slow down 
after a few years of strong growth? (b) Risk to VinFast revenues, ~22% of the overall 
revenues (c) Anchor client performance, ~32% of the overall revenues (d) exposure 
to mechanical offerings in the automotive vertical (e) Client concentration high – top 
5 clients contribute ~60% of the overall revenues in FY23 (f) service offerings overlap 
between TCS, Tata Elxsi & TTL. We have discussed these points in our report.

Investment Thesis 


Well positioned to benefit from automotive exposure – Over the last two years, 
ER&D spends from auto vertical has been healthy despite slowdown seen in other 
verticals – seen in case of peers (Figure 2). OEMs in automobile manufacturing 
have in the past rationalized their R&D budgets due to unfavorable macro 
environment to reduce costs but given the strategic nature of the product 
development, OEMs continue to invest in product development in the last 2 years. 
New technologies are disrupting the automotive sector with increased ER&D 
complexity, requiring specialized support. Global automotive companies are 
increasing their R&D investments across the broader theme of ‘ACES’ technologies 

– autonomous, connected, electrification and shared. The shift to alternative 
propulsion systems and specifically electric vehicles has enabled this 
transformation. TTL offers a one stop platform for automotive OEMs to meet new 
engineering needs across the value chain. 
TTL’s end-to-end solutions for EV development, manufacturing and after-sales 
services are designed to help OEMs develop competitive EVs while maintaining a 
balance among cost, quality, and timelines. The first step in creating EVs is a 
compelling vehicle concept and engineering design. The company has a suite of 
product engineering solutions including outsourced turnkey EV development, 
product benchmarking, electric vehicle modular platform (eVMP), for accelerating 
product development timelines and the light-weighting framework can help OEMs 
develop products within competitive timelines. Further, the suite of omnichannel 
after-sales solutions provided by the company powered by the “Power of 8” 
platforms can help OEMs engage with their EV customers early and manage the 
entire customer journey effectively.

Tata Group / Tata Motor parentage – TTL benefits from the track record, 
reputation, and experience of its promoter TML, which is part of the Tata Group. 
The Tata Group is one of the leading business conglomerates in India, with a 
heritage of over 100 years, comprising more than 28 equity listed companies 
across multiple verticals such as technology, steel and automotives. The Tata 
Group was recognized as the most valuable Indian brand in 2022 in the Brand 
Finance India 100 2022 report. 


We have seen parentage benefit in case of LTIMindtree (erstwhile L&T Infotech) & 
LTTS from its parent company, L&T Group. We believe Tata Group could help TTL 
acquire client relationships. One such vertical, in our view, where TTL could 
leverage its parent relationship is in the aerospace vertical – can TTL leverage Tata 
group relationship to get into other aerospace companies? – something to monitor 
going forward. 

Focus on other verticals particularly aerospace vertical – Apart from Automotive 
vertical, TTL has exposure to other verticals – Aerospace and Transportation and 
Construction Heavy Machinery (TCHM) verticals. 

In the TCHM vertical, the company provides service to equipment manufactures on 
an extensive range of products, including earth moving and construction 
equipment, mining, agricultural and forestry heavy machinery. Innovation in the 
TCHM industry typically lags behind the automotive sector innovation by three to 
five years. The company expects key trends like electrification, connected 
equipment and carbon footprint reduction will drive the TCHM industry in the 
coming years. TTL is well positioned to benefit from the trends when it plays out, in 
our view. 

In the aerospace vertical, TTL helps global aerospace companies to design, 
engineer and validate aircrafts using advanced processes, tools and technologies 
to manage the clients’ capacity utilization, product quality, operations and 
maintenance costs, and safety and security. Traditionally, this has included 
product and tooling design, interiors and seating layouts and enterprise

Marquee set of clients – TTL has a diversified global presence across Asia Pacific, 
Europe and North America and partners with many of the largest manufacturing 
enterprises in the world. As of 1HFY24, the company clients comprise more than 35 
traditional automotive OEMs and tier-1 suppliers and more than 12 new energy 
vehicle companies. Its client portfolio includes anchor clients, TML and JLR, 
leading traditional OEMs like Airbus, McLaren, Honda, Ford, and Cooper Standard 
and tier 1 suppliers as well as new energy vehicle companies such as VinFast, 
among others such as Cabin Interiors and Engineering Solutions, ST Engineering 
Aerospace. The combination of anchor clients, traditional OEMs and new energy 
vehicle companies provides a balanced mix of stability and growth, with revenue 
stability and further growth opportunities from anchor clients and traditional 
OEMs and significant growth opportunities with new energy vehicle companies. 
We believe that there is significant opportunity within its current client base to 
increase the use of its solution offerings and further develop deeper, long-term 
strategic engagements. 


In addition, given the high concentration of ER&D spend among select automotive, 
aerospace and TCHM companies globally, TTL can target large spenders in these 
industries, devoting substantial time and resources to cultivating relationships. 
Recently it has also been empaneled by Airbus which the company expects to 
become strong avenue of growth going forward.

Key Focus Areas 

Some of the OEMs have deferred/slowed their capex plans on electric vehicle – 
has the ER&D spends in automotive vertical peaked out? 


Warren Harris, CEO, Tata Technologies – “As we move into 2024, a little bit of 
caution is starting to permeate the sectors that we are operating in, specifically 
automotive. But because of the existential challenge that most of our customers 
are grappling with as the industry transitions from a predominantly internal 
combustion engine focus to the move to alternative propulsion systems and 
electrification and the move to connected services. We continue to expect 
investment to be strong and we think that that will signal significant tailwinds and 
demand that we expect to intersect with. 

Right now, based upon what we have seen towards the end of the calendar year I 
think there is a little bit more caution as we enter into 2024. But we will have full 
visibility in the sort of February-March time frame. So as of now, there will be more 
of the same although the sentiment is starting to decline a little bit and we will be 
able to quantify that in the early part of fiscal Q4”. (Economic Times, 19 Dec 2023) 


Our view – ER&D spends from automotive vertical has been healthy in the last two 
years given the shift towards EVs. OEMs were forced to invest else lose out to 
competitors. Going forward, we expect the ER&D spends from automotive vertical 
to continue to be healthy but the pace of spend may slow a bit compared to last 2 
years as OEMs focus on realizing the benefit of the investment done in the past 2 
years and reprioritize their spends.

For Tata Technologies, ~22% of the revenues in FY23 came from Vietnam 
geography that is majorly related to VinFast. Is there a risk to revenues from 
VinFast going forward? 

Warren Harris, CEO, Tata Technologies – “The situation in Vietnam is very 
consistent with what we see in terms of the life cycle of many new energy vehicle 
companies. They invest in products and then they pivot towards building 
recognition for the brand and building and selling products. We expect that to play 
out at VinFast. But I would say that they are investing in additional manufacturing 
capacity here in the United States. And we certainly expect that to be the catalyst 
for future investment in new products as we enter the new calendar year. We see a 
downturn in the next couple of quarters, but we expect that to improve as we move 
through calendar year 2024. Outside of that, the traditional OEMs continue to be 
relatively consistent and predictable. We have been very deliberate in terms of the 
customers that we target and work with. And so, we do not see any structural 
change or shift to the momentum that we have established.” (Economic Times, 19 
Dec 2023)

Revenue growth – Overall revenue growth for TTL will be dependent on the 
following key parameters – (a) growth from anchor clients (b) VinFast contribution 
going forward (c) Education business performance and (d) benefit from client 
mining and client hunting given the healthy tech spends in the automotive vertical. 
Our views on these parameters are below. 

• 
Growth from 
anchor 
clients – Revenues from anchor clients have remained flat 
over FY20-23. However, in 1HFY24, anchor clients grew ~17% over 2HFY23. 
Going forward, we expect TTL to benefit from their anchor clients as the 
company remains a strategic partner to the anchor clients. We are building in 
~35% / ~20% yoy growth from anchor clients in FY24E / FY25E – revenue 
contribution from anchor clients (% of overall revenues) will increase from ~32% 
in FY23 to ~39% in FY25E. 
• 
VinFast performance 
– TTL was engaged by VinFast across full vehicle turnkey 
programs for their VF6 and VF7 models resulting in significant growth for Tata 
Tech, ~200% CAGR over FY20-23. Going forward, as these full vehicle turnkey 
programs are behind and the company focuses largely on maintenance of these 
vehicles, revenues from VinFast will likely decline for TTL as seen in 1HFY24 – 
revenues from Vietnam geography declined ~26% from 2HFY23 to 1HFY24. In 
that context, we expect revenues from VinFast to decline by 30% / 20% in 
FY24E / FY25E – revenue contribution (% of overall revenues) from VinFast will 
decrease from ~22% in FY23 to ~9% in FY25E, also given the good growth in 
other parts of the business. 
• 
Education business 
– TTL education business focuses on addressing academia 
and corporate skilling requirements by leveraging their manufacturing domain 
knowledge and the iGetIT offering. The company has expanded the offering to 
develop curriculum and build dedicated innovation labs to train next generation 
skills and capabilities required by the global manufacturing industry. The 
company was able to leverage the engagements to create engagements with 
State Governments for industrial training institute (ITI) upgradation and further 
upskilling. Going forward, revenues from Education vertical will depend on the 
execution of the deals won by TTL – see Appendix. Hence, we are building in 
revenue growth of ~58% yoy in FY24E on back of Tamil Nadu deal getting 
executed and growth of ~33% yoy in FY25E given decent orderbook. 
• 
Benefit 
from client mining and client hunting – There are two parts here – (a) 
client mining – revenues from other clients (excluding anchor clients and 
VinFast) and (b) client hunting – similar to Airbus deal. Net-net, we are building 
in ~15% / ~19% yoy growth from these in FY24E / FY25E given resilience of tech 
spends in the automotive vertical and ramp up of Airbus account. 
Combination of above, results in overall growth of ~15% / ~15% in FY24E / FY25E 
for the company.

Valuations 

How to look at valuations for Tata Technologies? – There are two ways to look at 
valuations – (a) mid-tiers vs large tiers – we have mentioned in the past that well 
managed mid-tiers will likely grow faster than large tiers in the medium term. In 
that context, mid-tiers will trade at a premium to large tiers (b) relative to peers – 
Another way to look at the valuation is by comparing TTL with a set of ER&D peers. 

Combination of above, we value TTL on a target P/E multiple of 42x Sep’25E as the 
future growth potential looks strong given the resiliency of tech spends in the 
automotive vertical. Our assigned multiple of 42x is at ~10% discount to KPIT/Tata 
Elxsi average multiple of ~47x Sep’25E – factoring in VinFast revenues 
uncertainties and client concentration. While we are constructive on TTL, 
valuations appear too expensive at current levels. Valuations are at 53x 1-yr 
forward Citi estimates. We initiate TTL with a Sell rating and target price of Rs1000 
(~10% downside). 

Bear-Base-Bull analysis – Our Bear-Base-Bull analysis suggests a price range of 
Rs855-1325 depending on various scenarios. In our base case scenario, we 
assume ~15% US$-term revenue growth in FY25 and USD/INR exchange rate 
stabilizing ~Rs83. Our “Bull” case assumes pickup in revenues, target multiple and 
further rupee depreciation to Rs85/US$ with a consequent margin tailwind. Our 
bear case scenario assumes some slowing in growth, reduction in target multiple, 
rupee appreciation to Rs81/US$ and margin pressure.

About Tata Technologies – Tata Technologies (TTL) is a global engineering services 
company offering product development and digital solutions, including turnkey 
solutions, to global original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and their tier-1 
suppliers. It has domain expertise in the automotive industry and leverages the 
expertise to serve its clients in adjacent industries, such as in aerospace and 
transportation & construction heavy machinery (TCHM). TTL is a pure-play 
manufacturing focused ER&D company, primarily focused on the automotive 
industry, and currently engaged with six of the top 10 automotive ER&D spenders 
and four of the 10 prominent new energy ER&D spenders in CY21, per the Zinnov

Company description 


Tata Technologies (TTL) is an engineering services company offering product 
development and digital solutions, including turnkey solutions, to global 
original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and their tier-1 suppliers. It has 
domain expertise in the automotive industry and leverages the expertise to 
serve its clients in adjacent industries, such as in aerospace and 
transportation & construction heavy machinery (TCHM). The company is part 
of the Tata Group. As a subsidiary of Tata Motors Limited (TML), TTL benefits 
from long-term relationships with both TML and JLR. The long-standing 
engagements with TML and JLR (collectively, known as “anchor clients”) have 
enabled the incubation of skills and capabilities that have assisted TTL 
pursue opportunities outside of the Tata Group. 


Investment strategy 


We rate Tata Technologies shares as Sell. TTL is an ER&D service provider and 
will likely benefit from (a) automotive exposure (b) Focus on other verticals 
particularly aerospace vertical (c) mining and hunting opportunities in 
marquee set of clients. In addition, Tata Group parentage will further help in 
our view. However, valuations appear too expensive at current multiples. 

Valuation 


Our target price of Rs1000/share is based on 42x Sep’25E EPS as the future 
growth potential looks strong given the resiliency of tech spends in the 
automotive vertical however set at a ~10% discount to KPIT/Tata Elxsi – 
factoring in VinFast revenues uncertainties and client concentration. We 
believe PE remains the most appropriate valuation measure given TTL’s 
profitable track record. 


Risks 


Key upside risks to our investment thesis that could sustain the shares above 
our target price include: (a) better than expected demand from automotive 
OEMs in the US/Europe (b) additional business from VinFast (c) any 
significant depreciation of the rupee against the USD/EUR/GBP, and (c) 
higher billing rates.

Investment Ratings: Citi Research investment ratings are Buy, Neutral and Sell. Our ratings are a function of analyst 
expectations of expected total return ("ETR") and risk. ETR is the sum of the forecast price appreciation (or depreciation) plus 
the dividend yield for a stock within the next 12 months. The target price is based on a 12 month time horizon. The Investment 
rating definitions are: Buy (1) ETR of 15% or more or 25% or more for High risk stocks; and Sell (3) for negative ETR. Any covered 
stock not assigned a Buy or a Sell is a Neutral (2). For stocks rated Neutral (2), if an analyst believes that there are insufficient 
valuation drivers and/or investment catalysts to derive a positive or negative investment view, they may elect with the approval 
of Citi Research management not to assign a target price and, thus, not derive an ETR. Citi Research may suspend its rating 
and target price and assign “Rating Suspended” status for regulatory and/or internal policy reasons. Citi Research may also 
suspend its rating and target price and assign "Under Review" status for other exceptional circumstances (e.g. lack of 
information critical to the analyst's thesis, trading suspension) affecting the company and/or trading in the company's 
securities. In both such situations, the rating and target price will show as “—“ and “-“ respectively in the rating history price 
chart. Prior to 11 April 2022 Citi Research assigned “Under Review” status to both situations and prior to 11 Nov 2020 only in 
exceptional circumstances. As soon as practically possible, the analyst will publish a note re-establishing a rating and 
investment thesis. Investment ratings are determined by the ranges described above at the time of initiation of coverage, a 
change in investment and/or risk rating, or a change in target price (subject to limited management discretion). At times, the 
expected total returns may fall outside of these ranges because of market price movements and/or other short-term volatility 
or trading patterns. Such interim deviations will be permitted but will become subject to review by Research Management. 
Your decision to buy or sell a security should be based upon your personal investment objectives and should be made only 
after evaluating the stock's expected performance and risk. 


Catalyst Watch Upside/Downside calls: 


Citi Research may also include a Catalyst Watch Upside or Downside call to highlight specific near-term catalysts or events 
impacting the company or the market that are expected to influence the share price over a specified period of 30 or 90 days. A 
Catalyst Watch Upside (Downside) call indicates that the analyst expects the share price to rise (fall) in absolute terms over the 
specified period. A Catalyst Watch Upside/Downside call will automatically expire at the end of the specified 30/90 day period 
(or earlier if the investment rating of the company is assigned Rating Suspended or Under Review status); the analyst may also 
close a Catalyst Watch call prior to the end of the specified period in a published research note. A Catalyst Watch Upside or 
Downside call may be different from and does not affect a stock's fundamental equity rating, which reflects a longer-term total 
absolute return expectation. For purposes of FINRA ratings[1]distribution-disclosure rules, a Catalyst Watch Upside call 
corresponds to a buy recommendation and a Catalyst Watch Downside call corresponds to a sell recommendation. Any stock 
not assigned to a Catalyst Watch Upside or Catalyst Watch Downside call is considered Catalyst Watch Non-Rated (CWNR). 
For purposes of FINRA ratings-distribution-disclosure rules, we correspond CWNR to Hold in our ratings distribution table for 
our Catalyst Watch Upside/Downside rating system. However, we reiterate that we do not consider CWNR to be a 
recommendation. For all Catalyst Watch Upside/Downside calls, risk exists that the catalyst(s) and associated share-price 
movement will not materialize as expected.
